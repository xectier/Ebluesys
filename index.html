The Hidden Cost of Paper Registers

Traditional attendance registers create unnecessary friction at every level of your institution.

Manual Entry

Teachers waste 10-15 minutes every period doing repetitive paper work.

Scattered Records

Registers stored in different classrooms and administrative files.

Delayed Visibility

Management only sees attendance hours or days after it occurs.

Repetitive Reporting

Staff spend hours copying numbers from registers into monthly reports.

Attendance Errors

Manual tallying leads to mistakes in monthly percentages.

Limited Insights

Paper registers store numbers, but yield zero actionable data.
