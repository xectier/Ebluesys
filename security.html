Data Governance

Attendance data is institutional data.

Student attendance records support everyday academic administration. Managing that information responsibly matters.

01

Access

Who can see it? Role-based permissions ensure only authorized users view relevant attendance information.

02

Control

Who can manage it? Administrators control the platform — users, classes, subjects, and attendance scope.

03

Visibility

Who can monitor it? Principals and management see institution-level summaries without operational detail.

04

Accountability

How is attendance managed across roles? Structured workflows ensure data follows defined institutional paths.
